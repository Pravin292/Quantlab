Near 0 means two periods are alike; 1.5 means the first is about 4.5 times as volatile (in mean square). The open is the most volatile period in both markets.

What these results do not show

  • They do not show that C3 has a 5% error rate on real data. That is still unverified.
  • The real samples are not independent: the same six stocks and source shaped the simulation design.
  • Higher volatility at the open is well known, so the rejections confirm it rather than discover it.
  • C3 is cautious and has less power than the old test. Neither real result was close.
  • The Phase 6 placement protocol is proposed and unapproved, and was not needed here.
Key terms
H1
Volatility is the same in the open, middle and close of the trading day.
T
The largest of the three period differences divided by its standard error.
C3
A fixed cut-off for T, chosen in advance from simulations. Above it, H1 is rejected.
False-alarm rate
How often a test rejects when volatility really is equal. Target 5%.
Gated cell
A simulated scenario with a pass limit: 5.5% (tier A) or 6.0% (tier B).
SE
Standard error: how uncertain an estimate is.
